# Future Feature Roadmap

## Now — highest daily value

1. POS fast lane — favorites, recently sold items, common quantities, and faster search
   alongside barcode scanning.

2. Parked sales — set aside one customer’s cart and resume it later without losing items.
3. Daily cash close-out — record opening cash, cash in/out, counted cash, expected cash,
   and the variance with a reason.

4. Physical stocktake — guided count by category, variance review, and reason-coded
   inventory adjustments.

5. Utang guardrails at checkout — show live balance, available credit, overdue status,
   and enforce the suki’s limit or require an owner override.

6. Collection queue — a clear list of overdue and near-limit suki, with one-tap partial/
   full payment recording and an optional local follow-up date.

## Next — strengthen control and replenishment

7. Safe voids, refunds, and corrections — reverse a sale through an auditable workflow that restores stock and correctly adjusts cash or utang.

8. Supplier delivery receiving — record delivered quantities, actual purchase costs, shortages, and supplier invoices when restocking.

9. Offline reorder suggestions — create a supplier-grouped shopping list from low stock, sales history, and target stock levels; the owner always confirms it.

10. Stock movement timeline — a simple “why did this quantity change?” view across sales, restocks, spoilage, returns, and manual adjustments.

11. Owner PIN for sensitive actions — protect price overrides, large discounts, voids, stock adjustments, and debt-limit exceptions on the shared device.

12. Customer credit statements — generate an offline receipt or PDF statement showing purchases, payments, and remaining utang.

## Later — useful once the fundamentals are reliable

13. Expiry and damaged-goods tracking — only for products where it matters, with near-expiry and write-off reasons to make losses visible.

14. Transparent local store insights — practical tips derived solely from on-device history: items that repeatedly stock out, dead stock, margin changes, and suki payment patterns.

15. Smarter but explainable credit profiles — suggest—not silently decide—credit limits from payment timeliness, balance, and overdue history. This builds on the README’s planned risk/payer profiles.

16. Shift tracking on one device — local cashier profiles or shift handovers so cash variances and corrections have attribution without introducing multi-device accounts.

17. Manual encrypted backup and restore — export/import the SQLite data through the device’s Files/share flow. No cloud account or automatic syncing required.

18. Offline price-label/barcode sheets — generate printable labels from the existing catalog for shelves and repacked tingi items.
