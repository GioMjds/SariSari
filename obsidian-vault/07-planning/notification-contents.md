# Future Push Notification Contents

> **Parent Index**: [[planning|07-Planning Index]]  
> **Related Vision & Companion**: [[00-Vision/project-vision|Project Vision]] | [[07-Planning/second-companion-profile|Second Companion Profile]]

I want my notification contents to be clear, concise, actionable, relevant, helpful, personalized, and serve as a little companion. This is for users to continue engaging with the app and to help them achieve their goals in their store or business workflow without being too pushy or annoying. I want to avoid generic notifications that are not relevant to the user, and instead provide them with useful information that they can act on.

---

## Notification Categories & Triggers

1. **Inventory Intelligence**
   - Example: *"Your [Product Name] stock may run out soon. Consider restocking to avoid missed sales."*
   - Priority: High | Actionable & relevant to inventory management.
   - Connected Feature Specs: [[02-Features/04-physical-stocktake|04-Physical Stocktake]], [[02-Features/09-offline-reorder-suggestions|09-Offline Reorder Suggestions]]

2. **Credit / Customer Reminders**
   - Example: *"₱150 credit/utang from [Customer Name] is due today. Please follow up to ensure timely payment."*
   - Priority: High | Actionable & relevant to suki credit management.
   - Connected Feature Specs: [[02-Features/05-utang-guardrails-at-checkout|05-Utang Guardrails]], [[02-Features/06-collection-queue|06-Collection Queue]]

3. **Sales / Business Summary**
   - Example: *"You made ₱[Amount] in sales yesterday. Keep up the good work!"*
   - Priority: Medium | Relevant to business performance & motivation.
   - Connected Feature Specs: [[02-Features/14-transparent-local-store-insights|14-Transparent Local Store Insights]]

4. **Daily Companion**
   - Example: *"Good morning! 12 products need attention today."*
   - Priority: Medium | Operational summary from the store companion.
   - Connected Persona: [[07-Planning/second-companion-profile|Second Companion Profile]]

5. **Operational Reminders**
   - Example: *"Your daily sales report is ready. Review it to stay on top of your business."*
   - Priority: Medium | Operational close-out prompts.
   - Connected Feature Specs: [[02-Features/03-daily-cash-close-out|03-Daily Cash Close Out]]
