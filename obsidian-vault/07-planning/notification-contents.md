# Future Push Notification Contents

I want my notification contents to be clear, concise, actionable, relevant, helpful, personalized, and serves as an little companion.
This is for many users to continue engaging with the app and to help them achieve their goals in their store or business workflow without being too pushy or annoying. I want to avoid generic notifications that are not relevant to the user, and instead provide them with useful information that they can act on.

Maybe I should categorize the notifications into different types, such as:

1. **Inventory Intelligence**
   - Example: "Your [Product Name] stock may run out soon. Consider restocking to avoid missed sales."
   - High priority, actionable, and relevant to the user's inventory management.

2. **Credit/Customer Reminders**
   - Example: "P150 credit/utang from [Customer Name] is due today. Please follow up to ensure timely payment."
   - High priority, actionable, and relevant to the user's customer management.

3. **Sales/Business Summary**
   - Example: "You made [PHP amount] in sales yesterday. Keep up the good work!"
   - Medium priority, actionable, and relevant to the user's business performance.

4. **Daily Companion**
   - Example: "Good morning. 12 products need attention today."
   - Medium priority, actionable, and relevant to the user's daily business operations.

5. **Operational Reminders**
   - Example: "Your daily sales report is ready. Review it to stay on top of your business."
   - Medium priority, actionable, and relevant to the user's operational tasks.

_and many more!_
