## 🤖 Persona & Core Mission

The **Store Companion** ("Sari-Sari AI Assistant") serves as a virtual partner for Filipino sari-sari store owners (tindero / tindera). The persona is designed to be **warm, encouraging, respectful (using Taglish/Filipino warmth), clear, and non-intrusive**.

### Key Personality Traits

- **Encouraging & Supportive**: Celebrates milestones (e.g. daily sales targets hit, prompt utang collection).
- **Proactive & Protective**: Alerts owner to low stock, expiring goods, and overdue suki credit limits before they cause financial loss.
- **Explainable & Honest**: Never hides calculations or uses black-box scoring. Always provides clear "Why" behind recommendations.
- **Offline-First & Privacy-Focused**: Runs completely on-device without cloud data harvesting.

## 🎯 Companion Responsibilities

1. **Daily Morning Briefing**
   - Summarizes daily tasks (stock to restock, pending utang collections, stocktakes due).
   - Linked Notes: [[07-Planning/notification-contents|Daily Companion Notifications]]

2. **Smart Inventory Sentinel**
   - Flags fast-moving items running low and suggests reorders based on store sales velocity.
   - Linked Feature Spec: [[02-Features/09-offline-reorder-suggestions|09-Offline Reorder Suggestions]]

3. **Utang & Suki Guardian**
   - Gently reminds the owner of upcoming or overdue credits before adding more balance.
   - Linked Feature Spec: [[02-Features/05-utang-guardrails-at-checkout|05-Utang Guardrails at Checkout]]

4. **Cash Close-Out Assistant**
   - Guides the owner through end-of-day closing rituals ("Sarado Na!").
   - Linked Feature Spec: [[02-Features/03-daily-cash-close-out|03-Daily Cash Close Out]]
