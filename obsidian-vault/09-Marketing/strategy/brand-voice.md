# Brand Voice

How SariSari sounds across every channel. The voice is the same; the _register_
shifts a little by platform (LinkedIn is a touch more measured, TikTok is a
touch punchier) but the personality does not.

## One-Line Voice

> Warm, friendly, indie-builder. Filipino-flavored when it lands, English when
> the audience expects it. Optimistic, building-in-public, never preachy.

## Voice in Three Adjectives

1. **Mainit** - warm. We like the people we serve. We root for them.
2. **Tapat** - honest. We say what the app does and what it does not.
3. **Magaan** - light. We do not take ourselves too seriously, but we take
   the work seriously.

## What We Sound Like

- A small-business owner explaining the app to another small-business owner
  over a cup of kapeng barako.
- A developer shipping in public who is genuinely glad their thing works
  today.
- A neighbor who happens to know a lot about inventory and utang ledgers.

## What We Never Sound Like

- A press release ("We are excited to announce...")
- A SaaS landing page ("Supercharge your workflow")
- A motivational poster ("Empower your dreams")
- A scolding tito ("Dapat lang naman...")
- A pitch deck ("TAM, SAM, SOM, moat, runway")

## Words We Use

- Store, tindahan, suki, kaha, paninda, benta, utang, bayad, resibo, stock,
  imbentaryo, shift, suki, sukiing
- "Works offline", "hindi kailangan ng internet", "nasa phone mo lang"
- "Walang bayad", "walang subscription", "single purchase" (when relevant)
- "Built for", "made for", "para sa"

## Words We Avoid

- "Synergy", "leverage", "disrupt", "unlock", "empower"
- "User", "users", "user base" (say _tindero_, _suki_, _may-ari_)
- "Onboarding", "activation", "funnel" (jargon for internal docs only)
- "Cloud", "sync", "API", "backend" as selling points (we are offline-first,
  these are not the brag)

## Tone Modifiers by Channel

| Channel   | Modifier                                                 | Why                         |
| --------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Facebook  | Friendly, conversational, longer captions OK             | Suks and tindero live here  |
| LinkedIn  | A touch more measured, story-driven, founders-as-readers | Builder / OFW audience      |
| Instagram | Visual-first, captions get short, captions get punchy    | Younger audience, scrollers |
| TikTok    | Fastest, hardest hook, captions are mostly burned-in     | First 2 seconds decide      |

## Filipino Phrases That Travel Well

These are safe to drop in captions for the local audience. Use them sparingly

- one or two per post, never as a crutch.

- _"Para sa mga sari-sari store na tunay na nagsusumikap."_
- _"Hindi mo kailangang maging techie para mag-track ng stock."_
- _"Ang kaha mo, malinaw. Araw-araw."_
- _"Utang tracker na hindi nagloloko."_

## The Litmus Test

Before publishing, read the caption out loud. If it sounds like a brochure,
rewrite it. If it sounds like a person talking to a neighbor, ship it.
