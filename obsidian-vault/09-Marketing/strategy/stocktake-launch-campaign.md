# Strategy: Stocktake Feature Launch

**Created:** 2026-08-10
**Owner:** @gio
**Target window:** Week of 2026-08-17 (ISO Week 33)

## Context

Stocktake is the next feature shipping in SariSari - guided physical count,
variance check, and reason-coded inventory adjustments. Spec lives at
`obsidian-vault/07-Planning/physical-stocktake-flow.md`. There is no external
launch yet; this is the first public wave.

## Goal

Make store owners and the builder audience aware Stocktake exists, get 50
real installs from store owners, and collect 5 substantive pieces of feedback
within two weeks of launch.

## Audience

- Primary: existing SariSari users (FB followers, beta list, word-of-mouth)
- Secondary: tindero / tindera who have heard of the app but never installed
- Tertiary: builder / OFW audience watching the channel for honest updates

## Core Message

> Stocktake used to take a whole day. Now it takes thirty minutes - and you
> know where the variance came from.

## Pillars / Themes

Pulled from [[strategy/content-pillars.md]]:

1. **Sahi Tips** (40%) - "3 signs you need a stocktake" carousel, "Why your
   counted stock never matches the books" explainer
2. **Build in Public** (25%) - "We rebuilt stocktake twice. Here is why."
   LinkedIn long-form, dev-rant TikTok
3. **Suking Kwento** (20%) - Beta tester story (anonymized) about how the
   variance check saved them from a P4,200 mistake
4. **Hari ng Harap** (15%) - The actual launch announcement across channels,
   one honest "what did not make it" line

## Channel Mix

- Facebook (primary): 3 posts over the week
  - Mon: tip - "3 signs you need a stocktake"
  - Wed: launch announcement (with a 30-second screen recording)
  - Sat: Suking Kwento post-mortem
- LinkedIn (secondary): 1 long-form
  - Thu: "We rebuilt stocktake twice" - founder-led
- Instagram (secondary): 2 Reels + 1 carousel
  - Tue: teaser Reel (15 sec)
  - Thu: launch Reel (30 sec, demo + numbers)
  - Sun: carousel (5 slides: how stocktake works)
- TikTok (discovery): 4 short videos
  - Tue-Fri daily, hooks vary (POV, mistake, did-you-know)

## Tone & Voice

Per [[strategy/brand-voice.md]]: warm, friendly, indie-builder. Filipino-first
on Facebook. Founder-measured on LinkedIn. Visual-first on Instagram. Snappy
on TikTok.

## Constraints

- **No "cloud sync" language** - this is offline-first and we want to remind
  people during the launch.
- **No fake numbers.** If we have 47 beta testers, we say 47. If we have 12,
  we say 12.
- **No press release tone.** Plain words. Friend-to-friend.
- **Coordinate timing** - launch post on all four channels lands within a
  12-hour window so momentum compounds.
- **Image of one specific real store** (with permission) for Suking Kwento.
  If we cannot get permission in time, use the spec mockup but be honest
  about it.

## Success Metrics

| Metric                                         | Target (2 weeks) |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| New installs attributed to campaign            | 50               |
| Substantive feedback messages (DMs / comments) | 5                |
| LinkedIn long-form post impressions            | 5,000            |
| Facebook launch post reach                     | 3,000            |
| Total combined video views                     | 20,000           |
| Top-of-funnel: new followers across channels   | 200              |

If we hit the install target but miss the impression target, that is the
better outcome - installs > impressions for a single-purchase product.
If we hit impressions but not installs, the messaging is too vague.

## Launch Sequence

1. **Mon (2026-08-17)** - FB tip teaser ("3 signs you need a stocktake")
2. **Tue (2026-08-18)** - IG Reel teaser + TikTok POV
3. **Wed (2026-08-19)** - FB launch announcement (full)
4. **Thu (2026-08-20)** - LinkedIn long-form + IG Reel (full) + TikTok #2
5. **Fri (2026-08-21)** - TikTok #3 (dev-rant: why we rebuilt twice)
6. **Sat (2026-08-22)** - FB Suking Kwento post-mortem
7. **Sun (2026-08-23)** - IG carousel (how it works) + TikTok #4

The week after launch: 2x posts in the same channels, all Suking Kwento or
Sahi Tips, no more Hari ng Harap. The product has to do the work from here.

## Open Questions

- Do we have the beta store owner's permission and photo in time for Sat?
- Should we do a single FB Live walkthrough instead of the launch post?
  (Risky - bandwidth is high, payoff is uncertain.)
- Is the LinkedIn post too long for our normal cadence? Acceptable for
  a launch week.
