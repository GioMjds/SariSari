# Content Pillars

The four buckets every post falls into. If a post does not fit any of these,
it does not get posted. The pillars keep the feed varied without scattering
into random content.

## 1. Sahi Tips (Small Store Tips)

**What it is:** Practical, one-line operational tips that a tindero can use
tomorrow. Stock rotation, basic bookkeeping, signs that a suki is about to
default, what to track during payday weeks.

**Why it works:** It is useful _before_ anyone has the app. Builds trust.
Positions SariSari as the source, not the seller.

**Cadence:** 2-3 per week across channels.

**Format:** Short post, single tip, one screenshot or one photo if any.

**Sample hooks:**

- _"Bago ka magpalit ng supplier ng softdrinks, ito ang dapat mong itanong."_
- _"3 palatandaan na overdue na ang suki mo (at paano i-handle nang hindi
  nakaka-offend)."_
- _"Stocktake na 30 minuto lang, hindi isang araw. Paano?"_

## 2. Build in Public (Sneak Peeks)

**What it is:** Behind-the-scenes of the app being built. UI mockups,
before-and-after screenshots, small wins, small fails, decisions we made
and why.

**Why it works:** Indie-builder audience. Makes the project feel alive and
honest. Other devs share this kind of content because it is rare.

**Cadence:** 1-2 per week.

**Format:** Screenshot + 3-5 sentence caption. Optional short screen
recording for reels / TikTok.

**Sample hooks:**

- _"Bago at pagkatapos ng Inventory screen. Linis, hindi lang maganda."_
- _"Tried to fit debt history into one screen. Learned something about
  density."_
- _"Offline sync is not a feature for us - it is the architecture."_

## 3. Suking Kwento (User Stories)

**What it is:** Stories from real store owners - ours, beta testers',
interviewed ones. Always anonymized unless they explicitly opt in. The
texture of the day, not the testimonial.

**Why it works:** It is the only content that makes the audience feel seen.

**Cadence:** 1 per week, ideally once a month with a recorded voice note.

**Format:** Quote + photo of the actual store if available. Always ask
permission. Always frame it as _their_ story, not _our_ product win.

**Sample hooks:**

- _"Ganito ang isang araw ni Aling Rosa (na ngayon, hindi na niya
  kailangang bilangin ang benta sa kahapon sa kanyang notebook)."_
- _"Ang pinaka-malaking utang na na-track namin ngayong buwan: P12,400.
  Sa loob ng isang suki. Iyan ang dahilan kung bakit may SariSari."_

## 4. Hari ng Harap (Launches & Updates)

**What it is:** New features, releases, milestones. Real progress, real
dates, real numbers when relevant.

**Why it works:** It anchors the timeline. Audiences come back for launches
because launches are when they decide to actually try the app.

**Cadence:** As needed, but never more than once per week and bundled when
possible.

**Format:** Brighter visual than other pillars. Clear list of what changed.
Honest list of what did not make it.

**Sample hooks:**

- _"Available na ang Stocktake - guided, may variance check, hindi na
  umaabot ng isang araw."_
- _"v0.4 na. Pinned sale to cart, mas mabilis na POS, 18 bug fixes. Full
  changelog sa comment."_

## Pillar Mix Target

For a typical month:

- 40% Sahi Tips
- 25% Build in Public
- 20% Suking Kwento
- 15% Hari ng Harap

If a month is heavier on one pillar, that is fine if it is intentional
(launch month = more Hari ng Harap). Just do not let Sahi Tips fall below
30% for two months in a row - that is when the feed starts feeling like
a changelog.
