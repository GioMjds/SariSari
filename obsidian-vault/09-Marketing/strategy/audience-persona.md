# Audience Persona

The people we are talking to, in plain words. SariSari serves many kinds of
operators, but the marketing voice has to pick a primary audience or it ends
up generic. This is that primary audience, plus a few secondaries worth
keeping in mind.

## Primary: The Neighborhood Tindero / Tindera

**Name:** Mang Bert (or Aling Nene - flip a coin, both are right).

- **Age:** 35-60, but the long tail skews older
- **Location:** Residential barangay, anywhere in the Philippines
- **Day-to-day:** Opens the store at 5:30 AM. Family helps out. Customers
  are neighbors. Suks is plural and they remember what each one owes.
- **Phone:** Mid-range Android, often 2-4 years old. Data is metered, wifi
  is intermittent, signal drops in their area sometimes for hours.
- **Apps they already trust:** Facebook (the whole store runs on Messenger
  group chats with suki), GCash, a calculator, maybe a notes app.
- **Apps they do not trust:** Anything that asks for a login, an email, or
  says "cloud sync".

### What they care about

- **Hindi nawawalan ng stock.** Out-of-stocks are the worst thing that can
  happen at the counter - that is lost sale _and_ a suki lost.
- **Suking hindi nakakalimot.** Utang that is forgotten is utang that will
  not be paid. Even just a list of who owes what is worth real money.
- **Kung magkano talaga ang kinita.** Hindi yung estimate, hindi yung feeling
  - talagang pera-pesa.
- **Pagsasara ng araw na malinis.** Buong araw ng benta, malinaw kung magkano
  ang nasa kaha.

### What annoys them

- "Sync your data" - they have no data worth syncing and no spare MB
- "Sign up to continue" - they will not
- Long lists of features that do nothing for them
- Anything that assumes a tablet or a barcode scanner they do not own

## Secondary: The Builder Watching

**Name:** Kuya Dev.

- **Age:** 22-38
- **Location:** Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, plus the diaspora
- **Day-to-day:** Knows what Expo, React Native, SQLite, offline-first
  mean. Probably has a side project. Reads Indie Hackers.
- **Phone:** iPhone or flagship Android. Always-on internet. Will try your
  app, break it, and write a longer review than you expect.

### Why this audience matters

They are the amplifier. They will not be the customer, but they will be the
one who posts about it on X, writes the dev.to article, and mentions it in
the React Native Discord. SariSari should make them smile. The code has to
be as honest as the product is.

## Tertiary: The OFW Who Funds The Store

**Name:** Ate Joan, working in HK or Riyadh.

- **Why they care:** They wire money home and they want to know the store is
  running well. They are not the user. They are the _reason_ there is a
  budget for a phone upgrade and an app subscription.
- **What they want to see:** Stability. Less drama. Visible progress without
  theatrics.

## Anti-Audience

These people are not the target, and trying to talk to them makes the
message worse for everyone else:

- Chain-store procurement managers (we are not at that scale, and the
  feature set does not fit)
- Investors looking for a B2B SaaS pitch deck (different product)
- People who want a cloud-first, AI-first, "everything-connected" platform
