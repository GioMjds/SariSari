## Caption

```text
We rebuilt SariSari's stocktake flow twice.

The first version was correct. It mirrored what the spec said - count by
category, write the count, system flags the variance, owner confirms with
a reason code. It was how a developer would design it.

It was unusable.

A sari-sari store does not have a clipboard, a barcode scanner, or the
luxury of walking the aisles in order. What they have is a phone, two
hands full of product, and thirty minutes between a delivery and the
next wave of suki. The v1 flow forced them to scroll through 80
SKUs in category order. Half of them would be wrong by the time they
got to the next shelf.

So we rebuilt it. The v3 flow lets the owner count out of order, save
in place, and resume if a customer walks in. Variance shows up at the
end, not during. Reason codes are pre-filled with the four most common
ones - the rest are one tap away.

The result is a stocktake that fits the way a store actually runs.

Two rebuilds. Two months. One feature that fits the user's hands.

Shipping next week.

#BuildInPublic #IndieHackers #FilipinoTech
```
