# Posting Pipeline

A running board of every post in flight. One line per post, link to the full
draft under `calendar/`. Move entries between columns as they progress. This
is the _now_ view - the calendar under `calendar/` is the _when_ view.

## Conventions

- Format: `- [YYYY-MM-DD] [topic] - [[calendar/YYYY-MM-DD-topic-channel.md]] (@owner)`
- `@owner` is whoever is drafting / reviewing.
- When a post moves to **Posted**, create the matching
  `metrics/YYYY-MM-DD-topic-channel-results.md` and link it here too.
