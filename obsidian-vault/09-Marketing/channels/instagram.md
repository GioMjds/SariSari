# Channel: Instagram

## Role in Mix

Secondary. Instagram is visual-first. We use it for the screenshots,
the carousel explainers, and the reels. The audience skews younger than
Facebook but still includes store owners and their kids.

## Audience on This Channel

- Primary: younger store owners (or their kids who set the app up for
  them)
- Secondary: Filipino indie / dev community, designers, curious
  observers
- Tertiary: diaspora looking at sari-sari culture

## Posting Window

- Reels: 6:00 - 9:00 PM is the safest window
- Feed: same window, Reels out-pull static posts by 3-5x right now
- Stories: any time, multiple per day is fine

## Caption Format

- Shorter is better. 80-150 characters for the punch, longer only if the
  topic earns it.
- First line is everything. Reels show ~30 chars before truncation.
- Hashtags in the first comment (cleaner than at the end of the caption).
- One CTA at the end ("DM us 'STOCKTAKE' for the link").

## Hashtag Set

Mix of broad and niche. 8-15 per post, in the first comment.

```text
#SariSari #SariSariPH #TindahanTok #PinoyBusiness
#FilipinoEntrepreneur #BuildInPublic #RNDev #Expo
#FilipinoDev #SmallBusinessPH #TindahangPinoy
```

Rotate. Save hashtag groups per pillar under [[calendar/hashtag-groups.md]]
(create as needed).

## Visual Specs

- Feed: 1080x1350 (4:5) is the sweet spot for vertical real estate
- Reels: 1080x1920 (9:16), captions burned in (85% of viewers watch muted
  in the first scroll)
- Carousels: 1080x1350 each, 5-8 slides max
- Stories: 1080x1920, save the last slide as the "DM the keyword" CTA

## What Works Here

- Before / after screenshots of the app
- 15-second Reels: one tip, one camera angle, one CTA
- Carousel explainers ("5 signs na overdue na ang suki mo")
- Reels of the actual product in motion (POS, stocktake, utang list)
- Reels where we walk through a build decision

## What Does Not Work Here

- Static image with a paragraph of text on the image (looks dated)
- Reposting TikTok watermarked content - we will never do this
- Polished stock-photo-y screenshots with no real app in them
- Talking head videos with no editing (we are not that brand)

## Sample Captions

> "Puno na yung notebook mo?" - text on screen
> "Same." - text on screen, then cut to app screen
> Caption: "Single purchase, walang subscription, nasa phone mo lang.
> Link sa bio. #SariSari #TindahanTok"

## Reels Formula

1. **Hook** (0-2 sec): one short line of text, large, on screen.
2. **Problem** (2-5 sec): why this matters to a tindero in one sentence.
3. **Demo** (5-12 sec): actual screen recording, no narration, captions
   burned in.
4. **Payoff** (12-15 sec): "Link sa bio" or "DM 'KEYWORD'".

## Engagement Patterns

- Reply to every comment in the first hour if possible. The first 60
  minutes are when the algorithm decides if the Reel travels.
- Pin the three strongest posts to the top of the grid.
- One Story poll per week ("Anong pinakamalaking utang na natrack mo
  this month? A. <500 B. >500"). Low-stakes, high-engagement.
