# Channel: Facebook

## Targeted FB Page: SariSari - Ang Iyong Tindahan Assistant

## Role in Mix

Primary. This is where Mang Bert and Aling Nene already live. Most replies,
most DMs, most sukis happening in Messenger groups. We are not building
audience from scratch here - we are meeting the audience where they already are.

## Audience on This Channel

- Primary: neighborhood tindero / tindera (see [[strategy/audience-persona.md]])
- Secondary: OFW family members funding a store back home
- Tertiary: Filipino indie-builder / dev community watching

## Posting Window

Best times (Metro Manila-ish, verify with own Insights once we are past 100
followers):

- 6:00 - 7:30 AM (before store opens, commute scroll)
- 11:30 AM - 1:00 PM (lunch break)
- 7:30 - 9:00 PM (after store closes, wind-down)

Default pick: **7:30 PM on a weekday** for tips, **10:00 AM on Saturday**
for launches.

## Caption Format

- 3-6 sentences is the sweet spot. Long enough to land a story, short
  enough to finish on the first thumb-stop.
- Filipino-first OK. Code-switching is normal here.
- 1-2 emoji max per caption, optional. We are not an emoji brand.
- 3-5 hashtags at the bottom, often in a separate paragraph so they read as
  quiet metadata, not as a banner.

## Hashtag Set

```text
#SariSari #SariSariPH #TindahanTracker #ParaSaTindahan
#UtangTracker #POSParaSaTindahan #PinoyDev
```

Rotate - do not post the same five every time. Pin the brand hashtag
`#SariSari` in every post.

## Visual Specs

- Feed image: 1200x1500 (4:5) - more vertical real estate, beats 1:1 in feed.
- Cover / link image: 1200x630.
- Reels / video: 1080x1920, captions burned in (most viewers watch muted
  first).
- File under 5 MB if uploading direct. Higher quality via Google Drive /
  Dropbox link if needed.

## What Works Here

- Screenshot of the actual app, even unpretty
- Customer story (with permission) - the single highest-engaging format
- Reactions polls / "pick one" questions with two options
- Short recipe-style listicles ("3 palatandaan na overdue ang suki mo")
- Going live for a 15-minute walkthrough of a new feature

## What Does Not Work Here

- Pure English posts with no Filipino touch (audience skims past)
- Posting a screenshot and forgetting to add a caption
- Long threads. If it is more than 200 words, it should be a blog post.
- Hashtag spam (>10 hashtags reads desperate)

## Response Patterns

- **DMs about features:** Reply within 24h. If it is a feature we have,
  send a short screen recording. If it is a feature we do not have, log
  it in [[strategy/feature-requests-from-comments.md]] (create as needed)
  and acknowledge without promising.
- **Comments disagreeing / correcting us:** Reply with substance. Never
  delete unless spam. We look better for having answered well.
- **Tagalog vs English in replies:** Match the commenter.
