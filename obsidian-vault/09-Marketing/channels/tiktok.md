# Channel: TikTok

## Role in Mix

Secondary, growing. TikTok is the highest-leverage channel right now for
discovery. Lower-effort videos can travel farther than polished Reels. We
treat it as a discovery layer - it feeds Instagram and Facebook growth.

## Audience on This Channel

- Primary: younger Filipino internet users who might never have heard of
  a sari-sari store POS but love the cultural texture
- Secondary: sari-sari store owners and their families
- Tertiary: international audience that finds the small-business angle
  novel

## Posting Window

- 7:00 - 10:00 PM local time is safest
- Weekday afternoons (1-3 PM) catch the lunch-break scroll
- Post at least 3-5 times a week to stay in the algo's good graces

## Caption Format

- TikTok captions are short and barely visible in-feed. Treat the
  on-screen text as the real caption.
- The first 2 seconds decide everything. A flat text intro will not save
  a slow start.
- 3-5 hashtags, in-caption. Pin "SariSari" to the first slot.

## Hashtag Set

```text
#SariSari #TindahanTok #FilipinoBusiness #PinoyTikTok
#BuildInPublic #SmallBusinessPH #SariSariPH #TindahanPH
```

## Video Specs

- 1080x1920 (9:16), 15-60 seconds is the sweet spot
- Captions burned in, large, high-contrast
- Vertical-first. Native. Do not re-export Reels with watermark.
- Bitrate: high. Low-bitrate uploads look terrible on big screens.

## What Works Here

- POV-style ("POV: ikaw ang tindera at may nag-utang ulit")
- Day-in-the-life of the dev / founder (voice optional)
- App demo at speed (cut every 2-3 seconds)
- Sound-on hooks (trending audio at low volume, voice over)
- Cultural specificity ("Bakit may candy jar sa counter ng bawat
  sari-sari store")
- Mistakes we made ("Pinilit namin gawing cloud-based. Hindi tumama.")

## What Does Not Work Here

- Polished 60-second ads that feel like TV commercials
- Music-only videos with no hook in the first second
- "Like and follow" begging
- Anything longer than 90 seconds unless it is an interview-style story
- Reposting IG Reels verbatim - TikTok deprioritizes cross-posted content

## Hook Library (rotate, do not repeat inside 2 weeks)

1. "Pano kung sabihin ko sa'yo..."
2. "Hindi n'yo alam pero..."
3. "POV: may utang na naman..."
4. "Quick tip para sa mga tindahan:"
5. "3 araw na akong walang tulog dahil sa..."
6. "Tapos na ang diskarte na 'yun."
7. "Bawal 'to sa school. (literal na bawal)"
8. "Hindi ito paid promo. Promise."

## Sample Scripts

### Script: "POV: nag-utang na naman"

```text
HOOK (0-2 sec)
  Text on screen, big font, no audio:
  "POV: may nag-utang na naman sa'yo"

PAYOFF (2-7 sec)
  Cut to: looking at notebook with crossed-out numbers
  Text on screen: "Last week: P3,200. This week: P4,800."

TWIST (7-15 sec)
  Cut to: phone screen showing SariSari's utang tracker
  Voice (calm, slightly tired but smiling):
  "Hindi ko na kailangan mag-text ng 'bayad na ba?'.
  Nasa listahan. Kung sino, magkano, kelan."

CTA (15-18 sec)
  Text on screen: "SariSari. Single purchase, walang subscription.
  Link sa bio."
```

### Script: "Build in public - one tiny decision"

```text
HOOK (0-2 sec)
  Camera on the developer's face, slightly tired.
  "Ginawa namin ang Settings screen 4 na beses."

PAYOFF (2-12 sec)
  Quick cuts of the 4 versions, 1 second each.
  No voice. Text overlays:
    "v1: lahat nandun, walang hierarchy"
    "v2: settings-by-feature, mahirap i-scan"
    "v3: 3 sections, OK"
    "v4: 3 sections + 'help' sa taas"

LESSON (12-18 sec)
  Back to face.
  "Lesson: hindi yung 'ano ang nandoon' ang mahirap. Yung
  'ano ang una mong makikita' ang mahirap."

CTA (18-20 sec)
  Text on screen: "SariSari. Link sa bio."
```

## Production Tips

- Phone-native filming. The cheaper it looks, the more trustworthy it
  feels.
- One external mic for voiceovers is worth it (under P1,500).
- CapCut or VN for editing. Both free, both fine. Avoid watermarks.
- Always export a clean version with no TikTok watermark for cross-posting
  to IG Reels.
